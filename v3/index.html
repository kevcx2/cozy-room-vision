How to read this

One axis at a time.

Every render holds the same room — short green wall with the stove, long green wall with two windows and a french door, white walls and ceiling, light-oak floor, inglenook-tight loveseat, 2400K pool-of-light, lived-in clutter. Only the named variable changes. That's how we isolate what you're choosing.

Couch
Upholstery — color, fiber, slipcover language.
Drape
Window mood — neutral linen, color drop, pattern.
Rug
Floor anchor — pattern density, palette weight.
Wall
Long white wall — clean, shelved, salon-hung.
Direction 01 · Library

The tenured professor falls asleep with a book on their chest.

Walnut-mantel electric stove on the short green wall. Oxblood velvet Chesterfield-roll loveseat tucked tight to the hearth. Brass banker's lamp glow. Faded Heriz under everything. Books, slippers, dog.

The bones we hold

Walnut-mantel electric stove with raw-stone hearth. Loveseat ~36" off the hearth, angled 15° in. Tartan dog bed at the hearth, slippers on the rug. Brass banker's lamp lit at 2400K, brass picture light over a small framed botanical, overhead off.

The variants below all sit inside this room. Pick what you love on each axis — they compose.

01 Couch Upholstery swap
Option A
Mohair · forest green
Plush mohair velvet in deep forest, melts into the green walls so the loveseat reads as part of the architecture. Most enveloping of the three.
Baseline
Velvet · oxblood
Crushed oxblood velvet with brass nailhead. The "fall asleep here" choice — the warmest possible heart for a green-walled room.
Option C
Linen · mushroom
Crumpled stonewashed mushroom-linen slipcover. Lightens the room, keeps the lived-in slump, lets the brass and Heriz do more work.
02 Drape Window mood
Option A
Velvet · oxblood
Heavy oxblood velvet drapes pull the loveseat color up the wall. Most theatrical — the room reads like a private library.
Baseline
Linen · mushroom
Heavy mushroom-linen drapes with a 2-inch puddle. Quiet neutral that lets the oxblood couch and Heriz be the heroes.
Option C
Wool · plaid
Heavy wool plaid drapes (heather greys + russet). Pure cottage-shooting-lodge energy. Strongest pattern note in the whole room.
03 Rug Floor anchor
Option A
Bokhara · burgundy
Repeating Bokhara guls in burgundy + indigo + cream. Tighter, more geometric, denser pattern than the Heriz.
Baseline
Heriz · faded
Faded Heriz Persian — large medallion, soft rust + indigo + cream. The classic library rug. Warm but not loud.
Option C
Tabriz · indigo ground
Antique Tabriz with indigo ground and ivory medallion. Deeper, cooler, more formal — pulls the room toward "study."
04 Wall Long white wall
Baseline

Option B

Salon-hung

Direction 02 · Japandi

Deep enough you forget what you came in for.

Matte-black freestanding stove on the short green wall. Sixpenny Neva loveseat tucked tight to the hearth, slipcover crumpled. Akari paper lantern glow. Layered jute and Beni Ourain. Sumi-e scroll. Restraint is the move.

The bones we hold

Matte-black cast-iron-look electric stove with raw-stone hearth pad. Live-edge oak mantel. Loveseat ~36" off the hearth, angled 15° in. Akari paper-lantern floor lamp behind, plug-in linen-shaded sconce, overhead off.

Same room. Different palette logic — quieter, more breathable, but every bit as warm.

01 Couch Upholstery swap
Option A
Indigo · boro
Deep saturated indigo-dyed cotton slipcover with a sashiko-mended boro patch. The most graphic, most Japan-blue of the three.
Baseline
Linen · pebble
Crumpled pebble Belgian-linen slipcover, Sixpenny Neva slump. The quiet hero — disappears so the texture and light can carry.
Option C
Bouclé · cream
Thick nubby cream bouclé. Softer, fluffier, more new — pulls the room slightly toward Scandinavian.
02 Drape Window mood
Option A
Linen · charcoal
Heavy stonewashed charcoal linen. Cools the room down — the dark drape pairs with the dark green walls for a deep, womb-like envelope.
Baseline
Linen · natural
Stonewashed natural-linen drapes, drawn three-quarters with a 2-inch puddle. Bright, breathable, lets the green walls read deeper.
Option C
Noren · shibori indigo
Indigo shibori-dyed linen noren panels with white organic resist. Most explicitly Japanese — graphic, hand-made, signature.
03 Rug Floor anchor
Option A
Kasuri · indigo ikat
Dark indigo-and-cream Japanese kasuri-pattern flatweave layered on jute. The strongest graphic floor note — pure indigo geometry.
Baseline
Jute + Beni Ourain
Oversized jute flatweave base, cream Beni Ourain low-pile under the front legs. Quiet, textural, neutral — the most "disappear" floor.
Option C
Tatami · banded
Tatami-style woven rush with dark indigo cloth banding. Most architectural — reads as floor structure, not decoration.
04 Wall Long white wall
Baseline
Clean · sumi-e
A single sumi-e ink scroll at eye level on the long white wall. Rest blank. Restraint is the move.
Option B
Shelved
Three live-edge oak floating shelves, sparingly populated — single stoneware vessel, hand-thrown bowl, cloth-bound book per shelf.
Option C
Wabi-sabi grid
Asymmetric grid of small frames — sumi-e fragments, indigo textile swatches, small ceramics on tiny brackets. Maximalist by Japandi standards.
